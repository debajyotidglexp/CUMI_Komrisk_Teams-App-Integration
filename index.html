Compliance help inside the app CUMI uses every day

From Teams, CUMI employees can ask what a regulation requires, see which compliances are due for them, get reminders before deadlines, and ask Legal when they need a clear answer. KomriskAI stays the official record; Teams makes its information easier to reach.

What CUMI's answers mean for the design

Six points from CUMI's responses, each translated into a design decision.

CUMI told usTeams is the daily workspace for messaging and meetings.
→
So the designDelivers everything as a Teams app: a chat for questions and answers, plus notifications. No separate portal or login.
CUMI told usOne free-text prompt should explain what a compliance means and also give pending counts and due dates.
→
So the designUses a single entry point that understands the question and draws on two sources: KAI for what a requirement means, KomriskAI for the employee's own counts and dates, combined in one reply.
CUMI told usEmployees should be reminded of pending compliances and see them in their calendar.
→
So the designGenerates reminders from KomriskAI due dates and writes private entries to each employee's Outlook calendar, kept in step with KomriskAI. Calendar access needs CUMI IT approval.
CUMI told usEmployees should get clarifications from Legal in the same environment.
→
So the designAdds an Ask Legal action on every answer that sends the question, the answer and its sources to a CUMI Legal channel in Teams, with the reply returned to the employee's chat.
CUMI told usTeams is for information and clarification only; compliance work stays in KomriskAI.
→
So the designIs read-only. The integration only reads from KomriskAI, which keeps the build smaller and the security review simpler. Answers link to the record in KomriskAI.
CUMI told usAll employees should eventually have access, introduced in phases; questions outside compliance should be declined politely.
→
So the designControls access by employee group so each phase can be widened without rework. Employees without a KomriskAI account can ask regulatory questions but see no personal compliance data. Off-topic questions get a polite decline.

KAI regulatory knowledgeWhat a requirement means, with source provision and effective date

Design rule 1Know who is askingPersonal data is shown only after the employee is identified and linked to KomriskAI.
Design rule 2Permissions stay in KomriskAIEmployees see in Teams only what they can already see in KomriskAI.
Design rule 3Facts from the recordCounts and dates come from KomriskAI as stored; the AI explains but never calculates them.
Design rule 4Every answer has a sourceNo source found means no answer, and an offer to ask Legal.
Design rule 5Stay on topicQuestions outside compliance are declined politely.
